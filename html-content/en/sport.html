Running

Theorems during the week, kilometers on the weekend!

Alongside my research in mathematics, one of my main hobbies is running. I really enjoy long-distance running, and for some time now, I've been taking part in marathons and trail runs, alternating between the asphalt and the great outdoors.

I love the endurance and the magnificent effort required for these long distances, but I also love the incredible atmosphere of major marathons.

Below, you'll find the list of races I've taken part in, along with my times: you'll come across personal bests driven by pure determination, but also much more colourful adventures where the weight of the kilometers is measured mostly in glasses of Beaujolais and shared pieces of cheese.
