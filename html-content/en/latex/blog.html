LaTeX Blog

I'm going to use this blog to put my thoughts on LaTeX into writing, but also (and especially) to vent my frustrations. I will take this opportunity to call out certain LaTeX users—who are total jerks—for their terrorist behavior, but also to wage a holy war to defend my typographical conventions and my choices in LaTeX.

Well, it won't be just that (at least I hope not). There will also be posts presenting brand-new features freshly discovered by the author. There will also be some highlighting gems regarding the disastrous implementation of certain packages. Other posts will cover the juicy debates that set the sixty-somethings of the LaTeX sphere ablaze. And still others will explore certain aspects of LaTeX philosophy.

In short, there will be a little bit of everything. Happy reading!
